Welcome to VoiceVista.

If this is your first time using VoiceVista,

you might be wondering:

What exactly is this app?

And how is it different from other navigation tools?

VoiceVista is designed to help blind and low vision users stay aware of the world around them through sound.

The project continues the ideas of Microsoft Soundscape,

and carries them forward as an independent app focused on accessibility and exploration.

But VoiceVista is not designed to replace your cane.

Your guide dog.

Your mobility training.

Or even your favorite navigation app.

Instead,

think of VoiceVista as adding another layer of information to your environment.

Imagine walking down a street.

A coffee shop is on your right.

An intersection is ahead.

A familiar landmark is behind you.

VoiceVista turns information like that into sound.

If something is physically to your right,

the announcement comes from your right.

If your destination is ahead,

you hear it ahead.

Over time,

this helps build a mental map of your surroundings.

Many people first install VoiceVista expecting traditional turn-by-turn navigation.

VoiceVista can help with navigation.

But its real strength is awareness.

It answers questions like—

Where am I?

What is around me?

Which way am I facing?

What places am I passing?

How far am I from where I want to go?

Some people use VoiceVista only when traveling somewhere unfamiliar.

Others leave it running whenever they are outside.

As you continue using it,

your relationship with the app may change.

At first,

you might only use the beacon.

Later,

you may start creating markers.

Eventually,

VoiceVista becomes something more ambient—

a quiet stream of information that helps illuminate your environment.

One important idea to remember.

VoiceVista works best when combined with your own wayfinding skills.

You remain in control.

VoiceVista simply provides additional awareness.
